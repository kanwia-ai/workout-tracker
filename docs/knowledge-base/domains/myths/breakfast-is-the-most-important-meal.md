# Myth: Skipping breakfast crashes metabolism / kills gains

## The myth (verbatim)
"Breakfast is the most important meal of the day." "If you skip breakfast, your metabolism slows down and you store everything as fat." "You can't build muscle without protein within 30 minutes of waking."

## Why the myth persists
- Cereal manufacturers in the 1960s-90s heavily promoted the "breakfast is essential" message. The framing entered mainstream nutrition advice.
- Observational studies long correlated breakfast-eating with leanness — but those studies confounded breakfast with overall health behaviors (people who eat breakfast also tend to exercise more, smoke less, etc.).
- "Eat early to start your metabolism" sounds intuitive but doesn't match the actual physiology.

## What the research actually says
1. **Tinsley & La Bounty 2015** (Nutr Rev): comprehensive review of intermittent fasting research. Alternate-day fasting and time-restricted feeding produce comparable body-composition outcomes to evenly distributed meals when total calories and protein are matched. No metabolic damage from skipping breakfast.
2. **Levitsky & Pacanowski 2013**: skipping breakfast does NOT cause compensatory overeating at later meals sufficient to offset the deficit. People who skip breakfast typically eat fewer total calories per day.
3. **Aragon & Schoenfeld 2013** (anabolic window — see [anabolic-window](anabolic-window.md)): meal timing has minor effects compared to total daily protein intake. The window for protein "around" a workout is hours, not minutes.
4. **Intermittent fasting protocols** produce equivalent muscle-building / strength outcomes to traditional eating schedules when total calories + protein are matched, especially in resistance-trained populations.

## The corrected understanding
- Total daily caloric balance and total daily protein intake are what matter.
- Meal timing has minor effects within reasonable bounds (4-5 protein meals/day vs 3 meals/day vs intermittent fasting all work).
- Skipping breakfast is fine. Eating breakfast is fine. Pick what you'll adhere to.
- The "starts your metabolism" framing is a misunderstanding — your basal metabolic rate doesn't depend on having eaten recently.

## Application in this app
- Copy must NOT include "fuel up before your workout" / "breakfast is essential" / "eat within 30 minutes of waking" framing.
- LLM nuance layer: if user asks about pre-workout food, give honest answers — "eat or don't eat before your workout based on what feels good. Performance is similar either way for short-to-moderate sessions in trained individuals."
- App should NOT add "breakfast log" or "morning meal" features that imply moral weight to breakfast.
