# Training frequency — 2x/week per muscle when volume is matched

## Claim

**When total weekly hard-set volume is equated, training a muscle 1×, 2×, or 3× per week produces statistically similar hypertrophy** (Schoenfeld 2019 meta-analysis, 25 studies). Frequency is not an independent growth driver — it's a **volume-distribution tool**. Higher frequency is useful only insofar as it enables more weekly volume, better technique practice, or higher per-set quality.

The Pelland 2025 meta-regression confirms: frequency has small effect on hypertrophy *separate* from volume, but a *consistently identifiable* effect on strength gain (more frequent practice → more skill acquisition → more 1RM).

**Practical default for hypertrophy: hit each muscle 2× per week.** Reasons:
- Allows each session to stay at productive volume (5–10 sets/muscle/session, the high end of per-session productive returns) without overshooting the session-level junk-volume threshold.
- Sessions are shorter and individual sets are higher quality (less accumulated fatigue per session).
- Provides two stimulus events per week per muscle, smoothing recovery and reducing peak DOMS.

## Nuance

- **Frequency 1×/week is fine if total weekly volume is low (<10 sets/muscle/week).** A user training 2 days/week full-body will hit each muscle 2× (because full-body). A user training 2 days/week as upper/lower will hit each muscle only 1×, but at low total volume, that's recoverable in a single session.
- **Frequency 3+×/week becomes useful when weekly volume is high (>16 sets/muscle/week).** Cramming 18 quad sets into one squat day is physically miserable, junk-volume-loaded, and recovery-killing. Splitting across 3 sessions × 6 sets per session is more productive.
- **Strength specifically benefits from frequency.** Practice on the lift is practice — neural skill acquisition. 5/3/1 hits bench 1×/wk; Sheiko hits bench 3×/wk; the latter generally yields faster strength progression at advanced levels. Pelland 2025 confirms.
- **Recovery capacity dictates ceiling.** Older adults, fat-loss phases, and high-stress periods reduce per-session recoverable volume — these contexts favor higher frequency × lower per-session volume.
- **The "bro split" (1 muscle per day, 5–6 days) is suboptimal but not catastrophic.** It violates frequency-2× when sessions ≥4/week could provide it, but it still produces growth if total weekly volume is sufficient. Position: don't recommend it as default, don't ban it if the user prefers it.
- **Novices benefit from higher frequency on compounds.** 3×/week full-body lets the novice practice the lift pattern frequently (skill acquisition phase). Standard novice programs (Starting Strength, StrongLifts) all use 3× full-body for this reason — see also `strength-rep-ranges`.

## What this contradicts

- **"You need to hit every muscle 6 days a week to grow."** Volume-equated frequency makes no difference; 6× is just a busier 2×. (Myth: `myth-everyday-is-better`.)
- **"Bro split (1 muscle / week) is optimal because the muscle has 6 days to recover."** Muscles don't need 6 days to recover from a single set. Volume-equated, this loses to 2× frequency primarily because per-session volume becomes junk-loaded. (Myth: `myth-bro-split-required`.)
- **"Once-a-week destruction sessions grow muscle better."** False; the meta-analytic ledger shows no benefit and likely some detriment (junk volume late in a 25-set chest session). (Myth: `myth-once-a-week-frequency-optimal`.)

## Application in this app

- **Weekly scheduler enforces 2× frequency per muscle whenever sessions_per_week ≥ 3.** This is a HARD rule (non-negotiable #3 in the master synthesis).
- **1× frequency permitted only when weekly volume for that muscle <10 sets** — typically only in 2-day-per-week full-body programs.
- **3+× frequency permitted when weekly volume >16 sets/muscle/week** — typically intermediate/advanced hypertrophy-focused mesocycles using PPL or ULUL splits.
- **Split selection by sessions_per_week**:
  - 2 sessions → Full-body
  - 3 sessions → Full-body (preferred for novices) OR Upper/Lower with 1× frequency (acceptable at low volume)
  - 4 sessions → Upper/Lower (2× per muscle naturally)
  - 5 sessions → Upper/Lower + arms/weak point day, or PPL with 1 repeat
  - 6 sessions → PPL or ULUL (2× per muscle), bro-split only if user explicitly prefers
- **For strength goal**: prefer 2–3× / week on each main-lift pattern (squat, hinge, press, pull). Novice strength programs use 3× full-body specifically for skill frequency.
- **LLM nuance layer**: when explaining the 2× frequency default, cite Schoenfeld 2019 meta (volume-equated, frequencies equivalent → use frequency to *distribute* volume). Frame for the user as "we're hitting your chest twice this week so you can do 6 productive sets each session instead of 12 miserable ones in one day."
- **Do NOT recommend** bro-split as a default. Do NOT claim hitting a muscle "every day" produces faster growth — that's not what the literature shows.
